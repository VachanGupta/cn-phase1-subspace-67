The first request tried both backends (about 1 s per refused connect, because of Wi-Fi queueing on the path to mac_vachan, still under the 2 s proxy_connect_timeout). The next request got an immediate 502 because both upstreams were inside their 10 s fail_timeout.

After restoring both: ports open again, and after nginx's 10 s fail_timeout the rotation is back (healthy at 14:09:46).

Layer and why

This shows where the edge ends and the backends begin. DNS worked (the name resolved to the edge), TCP worked (handshake with the edge), and TLS worked (the edge holds the certificate and key and terminates TLS), so the client's secure channel to the edge was fine. Only behind the edge did everything fail: nginx's connects to both upstreams were refused, so nginx generated the error response itself, 502 Bad Gateway ("I am a gateway, and the servers behind me gave me no valid response"). A 502 from server: nginx after a successful handshake points the diagnosis at the upstreams, not at DNS, TCP or TLS.
